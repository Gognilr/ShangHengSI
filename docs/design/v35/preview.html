1本规格书适用于 HX 系列卧式离心泵，规定了产品的性能参数、材料要求与检验方法。

This specification applies to HX series horizontal centrifugal pumps and sets out the performance parameters, material requirements and inspection methods.

2泵体采用 HT250 灰铸铁，叶轮采用 304 不锈钢；机械密封的使用寿命不低于 8,000 小时。

The pump casing shall be made of HT250 grey cast iron and the impeller of 304 stainless steel; the service life of the mechanical seal shall be no less than 8,000 hours.

3出厂前应逐台进行水压试验，试验压力为最大工作压力的 1.5 倍，保压 10 分钟无渗漏。

Each pump shall undergo a hydrostatic test before delivery at 1.5 times the maximum working pressure, held for 10 minutes

4质保期为设备验收合格之日起 24 个月。质保期内因制造缺陷造成的损坏，由供方免费维修或更换。
